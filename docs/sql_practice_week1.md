# SQL Practice Week 1

## Practice queries

1. Select the total revenue for APAC enterprise customers in Q4 2025.
2. Select the total marketing spend for APAC enterprise customers in Q4 2025.
3. Select the total churn count for APAC enterprise customers in Q4 2025.
4. Compare Q3 2025 and Q4 2025 revenue for APAC enterprise customers.
5. Compare Q3 2025 and Q4 2025 churn for APAC enterprise customers.
6. List support tickets that mention pricing objections in APAC during Q4 2025.
7. Count support tickets by category for APAC enterprise customers in Q4 2025.
8. Show the top 5 customers by revenue in APAC for Q4 2025.
9. Identify customers with increased churn risk in APAC for Q4 2025.
10. Join revenue and marketing spend by quarter for APAC enterprise customers.
11. Find all customers with negative revenue momentum in APAC Q4 2025.
12. Summarize support escalation volume by region for enterprise customers.

Tables used in practice:
