# Week 1 Study Notes

## metric
A measurable business value such as revenue, churn, or marketing spend.

## dimension
A categorical axis used to slice metrics, such as region, segment, or quarter.

## filter
A condition used to narrow data, such as APAC, Q4 2025, or enterprise customers.

## KPI
A key performance indicator used to track business progress.

## revenue
Income generated from customers or products.

## recognized revenue
Revenue that has been recognized under accounting and reporting rules.

## pipeline
Potential future revenue opportunities that are not yet recognized.

## bookings
The value of deals that are secured but may not yet be recognized as revenue.

## churn
The rate or count of customers lost over a period.

## retention
The share of customers kept over a period.

## marketing spend
The money invested in campaigns or demand generation.

## API
A defined interface for software components to communicate.

## backend
The server-side logic that handles queries, processing, and validation.

## frontend
The user-facing UI layer, such as a Streamlit app.

## execution trace
The sequence of steps taken from query intake to final answer.

## hallucination
A false or unsupported claim introduced by the system.

## grounding
The practice of basing answers on evidence from data or documents.

## faithfulness
Whether the answer stays aligned with the source evidence.

## Recall@K
A retrieval metric that measures whether the correct evidence appears in the top K results.

## MRR
Mean Reciprocal Rank, a metric that rewards correct evidence appearing earlier.

## SQL execution accuracy
A measure of whether the generated or selected SQL returns the correct data.
